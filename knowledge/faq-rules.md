# FAQ Rules

## FAQ Structure

- Include 5-7 FAQ questions per article
- Questions should be common user queries
- Answers should be concise but comprehensive
- Use clear, direct language

## FAQ Guidelines

### Questions
- Start with "What," "Why," "How," "When," or "Is"
- Use natural language (how real users would ask)
- Include keywords where appropriate
- Cover different aspects of the topic

### Answers
- Keep answers between 50-150 words
- Provide actionable advice
- Use bullet points for lists
- Include links to related content
- Reference the main article when appropriate

## Example FAQ Format

**Q: What causes knee pain in seniors?**

A: Knee pain in seniors can have several causes, including osteoarthritis, injury, inflammation, or overuse. Age-related wear and tear on the joints is a common factor, as cartilage naturally thins over time. Other contributing factors may include obesity, lack of exercise, or previous knee injuries.

## Best Practices

- Place FAQ section near the end of the article
- Use consistent formatting throughout
- Update FAQs based on user feedback
- Include "Related Questions" at the end
- Make FAQs easy to scan and read

## Common FAQ Topics

For health articles, common FAQ topics include:
- Causes and risk factors
- Symptoms and diagnosis
- Treatment options
- Prevention strategies
- When to see a doctor
- Lifestyle changes
- Common misconceptions

# Medical Guidelines

## General Principles

- This content is for informational purposes only
- Always advise readers to consult a healthcare professional
- Do not provide specific medical advice or diagnosis
- Use cautious language when discussing treatments

## Safety Rules

1. **Avoid absolute claims** - Use "may help," "can contribute to," or "is associated with" instead of "cures," "treats," or "prevents"

2. **Disclose limitations** - Acknowledge that individual results may vary

3. **Avoid fear-mongering** - Present information objectively without causing unnecessary anxiety

4. **Be evidence-based** - Base claims on generally accepted medical principles

5. **Avoid conflicts of interest** - Do not promote specific products or brands

## Common Medical Claims to Avoid

- ❌ "Walking cures arthritis"
- ✅ "Walking may help reduce arthritis symptoms"

- ❌ "This supplement prevents heart disease"
- ✅ "Some studies suggest this supplement may support heart health"

- ❌ "You should take this medication"
- ✅ "Consult your doctor about whether this medication is right for you"

## Disclaimer Requirements

Every article must include a disclaimer at the end:

> "This article is for informational purposes only and should not be taken as medical advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional before making any changes to your health routine."

## Special Considerations for Seniors

- Be mindful of age-related limitations
- Recommend gentle exercises and gradual changes
- Emphasize consulting healthcare providers before starting new routines
- Address common concerns like mobility, balance, and medication interactions

# Writing Tone

## Tone Options

### Friendly
- Warm and approachable
- Use conversational language
- Address readers directly ("you")
- Include empathy and understanding
- Avoid overly technical terms

### Professional
- Expert and knowledgeable
- Use proper terminology
- Formal but accessible
- Cite medical principles
- Maintain credibility

### Authoritative
- Confident and trustworthy
- Use strong, clear language
- Reference established guidelines
- Position as a reliable source
- Avoid speculation

### Conversational
- Casual and engaging
- Use everyday language
- Like a conversation with a friend
- Include anecdotes when appropriate
- Keep sentences short

### Empathetic
- Understanding and compassionate
- Acknowledge challenges and struggles
- Validate feelings
- Provide encouragement and support
- Focus on solutions and hope

## Tone Consistency

- Maintain the same tone throughout an article
- Match tone to the topic (e.g., more empathetic for chronic pain)
- Match tone to the audience (e.g., friendly for general readers)

## Avoid These

- Overly casual slang
- Technical jargon without explanation
- Fear-mongering or alarmist language
- Patronizing or condescending tone
- Pushy sales language

## Example Tone Guidelines

For senior health content:
- Use empathy and understanding
- Acknowledge age-related challenges
- Offer practical, actionable advice
- Encourage and empower readers
- Maintain respect and dignity

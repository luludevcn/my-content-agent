# Style Guide

## General Guidelines

- Write in clear, accessible English
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences maximum)
- Use active voice whenever possible
- Avoid jargon; explain medical terms when used
- Use bullet points and numbered lists for readability
- Maintain a consistent tone throughout each article

## Article Structure

Every article must follow this fixed structure:

1. **What is it?** - Definition and general overview
2. **Common causes** - List and explain causes
3. **Symptoms** - Describe symptoms clearly
4. **When to see a doctor** - Warning signs and recommendations
5. **What may help** - Evidence-based treatments, remedies, and lifestyle changes
6. **Key takeaway** - Summary of the most important points

## Heading Hierarchy

- H1: Main article title
- H2: Main sections (What is it?, Common causes, etc.)
- H3: Subsections within main sections
- H4: Minor subtopics (rarely used)

## Language Tips

- Use "may help" instead of "cures" or "treats"
- Use "can be" instead of "is" when describing symptoms
- Avoid absolute statements
- Be empathetic but professional
- Address the reader directly when appropriate

## Formatting Rules

- Use bold for emphasis (not ALL CAPS)
- Use italics for medical terms on first mention
- Use code blocks for technical terms
- Use quotes for direct advice
- Include relevant statistics when available

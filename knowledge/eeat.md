# E-E-A-T Guidelines

## Expertise

### Author Expertise
- Authors should have relevant credentials
- Include author bio with qualifications
- Reference professional experience
- Cite relevant certifications or education

### Content Expertise
- Demonstrate deep knowledge of the topic
- Provide comprehensive coverage
- Include detailed explanations
- Address complex topics thoroughly

## Experience

### First-Person Experience
- Include personal stories when relevant
- Share practical insights
- Provide real-world examples
- Reference case studies or patient experiences

### Practical Advice
- Offer actionable recommendations
- Include step-by-step guidance
- Provide tips from experience
- Share lessons learned

## Authoritativeness

### Sources and Citations
- Cite reputable medical organizations
- Reference peer-reviewed studies
- Link to authoritative websites
- Include statistics from trusted sources

### Credibility Signals
- Include about page with team credentials
- Display awards or recognitions
- Include media mentions
- Show community trust indicators

## Trustworthiness

### Transparency
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Clearly label sponsored content
- Explain methodology when appropriate
- Be honest about limitations

### Accuracy
- Fact-check all claims
- Correct errors promptly
- Provide balanced perspectives
- Avoid misleading information

### Security
- Use secure website practices
- Protect user data
- Include privacy policy
- Use HTTPS

## Additional Trust Signals

- Include contact information
- Provide easy ways to report errors
- Include testimonials or reviews
- Show social proof
- Update content regularly
- Maintain consistent branding

## For Health Content

- Include medical disclaimer
- Clearly state limitations
- Recommend consulting professionals
- Avoid making diagnostic claims
- Use evidence-based information
- Acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate
